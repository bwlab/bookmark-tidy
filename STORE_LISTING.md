## English (en)

**Short description** (≤132 chars)
Clean up and organize your Chrome bookmarks with a fast, modern interface. 100% local, sync-safe.

**Detailed description**
Bookmark Tidy bwlab helps you take control of a messy bookmarks bar.

FEATURES
- Full Bookmark Manager replacement with sidebar tree, instant search, drag & drop, and folder management
- Keyboard-first navigation (search, open, rename, delete — no mouse needed)
- Available in 19 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Hindi and Russian
- Light/dark theme

PRIVACY
Everything runs locally on your device through Chrome's official `chrome.bookmarks` API. No data is collected, transmitted, or sold. If Chrome Sync is enabled, changes sync through Google's own infrastructure — never through third-party servers.

Open source (MIT license): github.com/bwlab/bookmark-tidy
